隐私政策

Google 登录与校园档案

您可不登录使用游客功能。主动使用 Google 登录后，Firebase Authentication 验证身份，并向网站提供内部 UID、邮箱、展示名称与头像。邮箱用于向您显示当前账号；已验证 UID 用于关联您主动建立的校园档案。本站不请求 Gmail、Google Drive 或联系人权限。

Firebase 管理 Google 登录身份与会话；应用不自行在 localStorage 保存原始令牌。学号仅为虚构展示编号，不作为身份认证凭据。GitHub Pages 提供网站页面，Cloudflare Workers 验证 API 身份，Cloudflare D1 保存您提交的昵称、学院、模拟成绩、论文、证书及成就等校园记录。

可见性与本机数据

新建档案默认私有。公开名片需要您单独明确同意，可以在账户设置中撤回。本机游客记录存于该浏览器，与云端档案分开；仅在您主动导入时复制到当前账号。共享设备上的本机记录可能来自其他使用者。

导出、删除与数据保留

可在 My ANU 的账户设置中导出或删除本人云端档案。删除需要近期 Google 验证与明确确认，只删除 ANU 校园档案及关联记录，不删除 Google 账号或 Firebase 身份。本机游客记录需另行清理；安全审计记录和私有灾备副本有独立保留边界，云端删除不代表这些记录立即清除。

运行与安全服务

网站使用 Firebase 进行身份验证，使用 Cloudflare 提供 API、数据库、Turnstile 及防滥用保护。安全处理可使用网络地址信息；写入配额和审计使用散列后的网络标识。Google Fonts 提供网站字体。访问这些服务时，服务提供方按其自身政策处理请求所需的网络信息。

使用条款

Absolutely Nothing University (ANU · freeze.edu.kg) 是虚构的讽刺与幽默项目。所有学位、学号、成绩和论文仅供娱乐，不具备现实学术或法律效力。

不得进行越权访问、注入攻击、自动化滥用或高频刷接口。云端操作受身份、所有权、挑战、限流和功能开关保护；维护或安全验收期间可暂停注册、论文投稿或证书登记。游客功能仍可独立使用。

Privacy Policy

Google sign-in and campus records

Guest features do not require sign-in. When you voluntarily sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication verifies your identity and supplies your UID, email, display name, and photo. Email identifies the current account to you; the verified UID links the campus profile you choose to create. ANU does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or contacts.

Firebase manages your sign-in identity and session. Application code does not manually store raw tokens in localStorage. Student numbers are fictional display identifiers, never authentication credentials. GitHub Pages serves the website, Cloudflare Workers verifies API identity, and Cloudflare D1 stores submitted campus records such as nickname, college, mock grades, papers, diplomas, and achievements.

Visibility and device data

New campus profiles are private by default. A public profile card requires separate, explicit consent, which you can withdraw in Account Settings. Guest records stay in the browser and remain separate from the cloud; they are copied to an account only after an explicit import. Device records on a shared browser may belong to other users.

Export, deletion, and retention

Export or delete your own cloud archive in My ANU Account Settings. Deletion requires recent Google authentication and explicit confirmation. It deletes ANU campus records, not your Google account or Firebase identity. Device records require separate cleanup. Security audit records and private recovery backups have separate retention boundaries and are not immediately purged when the cloud archive is deleted.

Operational and security services

ANU uses Firebase for authentication and Cloudflare for its API, database, Turnstile, and abuse protection. Security processing may use network address information; write quotas and audit records use hashed network identifiers. Google Fonts supplies website fonts. These providers process network information needed for requests under their own policies.

Terms of Use

Absolutely Nothing University (ANU · freeze.edu.kg) is a fictional parody project, not an accredited institution. All degrees, IDs, grades, and papers are for entertainment and have no real academic or legal effect.

Unauthorized access, injection attacks, automated abuse, and excessive requests are prohibited. Cloud operations enforce identity, ownership, challenges, rate limits, and feature switches. Registration, paper submissions, or diploma registration may be paused for maintenance or security acceptance; guest features remain independently available.
